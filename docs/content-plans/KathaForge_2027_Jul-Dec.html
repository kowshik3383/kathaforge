Publish times and the first month’s test

January pilot: Alternate weekday Shorts between 6:30 PM and 8:00 PM by plan week. Test Saturday Shorts at 11:00 AM and 6:30 PM. Keep Sunday episodes at 6:30 PM as a baseline.

From 29 January: Use 7:30 PM on weekdays, 11:00 AM for Saturday Shorts, and 6:30 PM for Sunday episodes until your data suggests a change. These are starting times for an India-based audience.

  1. When available, check YouTube Studio → Analytics → Audience → When your viewers are on YouTube.
  2. Compare similar Shorts across slots using first-24-hour views and Stayed to watch. For Sunday episodes, check first-24-hour views and retention separately.
  3. Review after four weeks and revisit monthly. Small samples and topic differences can obscure time effects.

Dates are editorial planning dates. Check local festival dates before locking time-sensitive uploads. Each weekly theme has six Shorts and one Sunday episode, with an extra year-end recap Short.
